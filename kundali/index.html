Kundli questions

Before you begin

How accurate does my birth time need to be?+

As close as you can get it. The Lagna changes roughly every two hours and the Navamsa every 10 to 15 minutes. A birth certificate or hospital record is best. If you are unsure, Chandan can rectify the time in a consultation.

Which ayanamsa is used?+

Lahiri (Chitrapaksha), the standard used by the Indian government's calendar and by most Vedic astrologers in India.

North or South Indian chart?+

The chart is drawn in the North Indian style, with the houses fixed and the signs moving. The calculation is the same either way.

Is my birth data stored?+

No. The calculation runs inside your own browser and nothing you type is sent to us. See the privacy policy for how consultation details are handled.

How accurate is the free calculation?+

Planet positions are computed with standard astronomical formulas and are accurate to within about a degree (the Sun and Moon to a few arc-minutes), which is enough for signs, Nakshatras and Dashas. If a planet or your Lagna sits right on a boundary, Chandan double-checks it with a full ephemeris in a consultation.
