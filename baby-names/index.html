Namkaran

A name that begins with the birth star.

Each of the 27 Nakshatras has four padas, and each pada is linked to a sound: Chu, Che, Cho and La for Ashwini, for example. The custom is to begin the child's name with the sound of their pada, or at least one from their Nakshatra.

The list here is short and chosen for clear meanings. Many families also use the syllable for a pet name and choose the formal name freely. The Nakshatra page lists the syllables for all 27.
