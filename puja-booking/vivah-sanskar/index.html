About this puja

The complete Vedic wedding ceremony, from Ganesh puja and kanyadaan to the saptapadi, with each vow explained in simple language so both families can follow along.

When it is done

  • ✦The wedding day, on a chosen muhurat
  • ✦Pre-wedding pujas such as Haldi or Mandap puja

What happens

  1. 1

    Ganesh puja

  2. 2

    Kanyadaan

  3. 3

    Panigrahan

  4. 4

    Saptapadi (seven vows)

  5. 5

    Sindoor daan and blessings

The exact sequence is confirmed with you in advance and adjusted to your family's custom.

Before the day

What to prepare.

The pandit confirms the full samagri list when the muhurat is fixed.

  • ✓Birth details of both, for the muhurat
  • ✓Names and gotras of both families
  • ✓The mandap set up at the venue, with space for the havan
  • ✓Mangalsutra, sindoor, garlands and the items for kanyadaan

Is this the right puja for you?

Chandan can check your chart first and confirm whether this puja will help.
