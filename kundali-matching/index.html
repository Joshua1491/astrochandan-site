How the score works

Eight kootas, 36 gunas.

Each koota compares one aspect of the two Moon signs and birth stars. Their points add up to a total out of 36.

The score is a starting point, not a verdict. A full match also weighs Mangal dosha, both seventh houses, the Navamsa and the Dashas each person will run after marriage.
