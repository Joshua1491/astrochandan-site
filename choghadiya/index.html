Choghadiya

The day and night divided into eight periods each, from sunrise to sunset and sunset to the next sunrise. A quick, traditional way to pick a good hour for a journey or a new start.

How it works

Seven kinds of time.

Daylight is split into eight equal parts, and so is the night. Each part, roughly an hour and a half, takes one of seven qualities in a fixed order that starts from the ruler of the weekday.

Families in Gujarat and Rajasthan especially use the Choghadiya for everyday choices: when to set out, sign a deal or open a shop. For major events, a full muhurat drawn from the Panchang is preferred.

ChoghadiyaQualityMeaning
AmritBestNectar. Good for any auspicious work.
ShubhGoodAuspicious. Good for ceremonies, study and marriage matters.
LabhGoodGain. Good for business, trade and starting work.
CharNeutralMoving. Good for travel and for anything that needs to move.
UdvegAvoidAnxiety. Traditionally avoided, though suitable for government work.
KaalAvoidLoss. Avoided for new starts.
RogAvoidIllness. Avoided for new starts.
