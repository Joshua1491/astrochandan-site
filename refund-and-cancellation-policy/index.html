Refund and Cancellation Policy

How rescheduling, cancellations and refunds work for consultations and pujas. Last updated 16 November 2026.

Plans change. This policy sets out clearly what happens if you need to reschedule or cancel a consultation or puja, and what happens if we have to.

Summary

1. Consultations

Rescheduling

You can move your consultation to another available slot free of charge if you let us know at least 24 hours before the booked time.

Cancelling 24 hours or more before

You will receive a full refund of the consultation fee.

Cancelling less than 24 hours before

Chandan will have set aside time and prepared your chart. You may either reschedule once to another available slot, or receive a 50% refund of the fee.

No-show

If you do not attend and have not told us beforehand, no refund is given. If something genuinely unavoidable happened, please contact us and we will consider it fairly.

2. Pujas

Cancelling 72 hours or more before

You will receive a full refund, less the cost of any samagri (puja materials) already purchased for your puja. We will tell you that amount.

Cancelling less than 72 hours before

Arrangements with the pandits are usually fixed by then. We will first offer to move the puja to another suitable date. If you prefer a refund, it will be the amount paid less samagri already purchased and pandit arrangements already committed, which we will explain to you.

After the puja

Once a puja has been performed, it cannot be refunded.

3. If we cancel

If we have to cancel a consultation or puja for any reason, you will receive a full refund, or a new date if you would rather keep the booking.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds are made to the original payment method, within 7 to 10 working days of being approved. Your bank or payment provider may take a little longer to show the amount in your account.

5. How to request a change or refund

Write to us through the booking form on this website, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 IST, with your name, the booking date and time, and what you would like to do. The time your message is received counts as the time of notice. We will confirm by phone or message.

6. Fairness

Astrology and puja results cannot be guaranteed, so refunds are not given on the grounds that a prediction or outcome did not match expectations. If you are unhappy with a service for any other reason, please tell us; we would much rather hear it and try to put it right.
