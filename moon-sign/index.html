Why the Moon

Jyotish reads the mind through the Moon.

Western astrology leads with the Sun sign. Vedic astrology gives the Moon equal or greater weight, because the Moon describes the mind, moods and instincts: how you experience your life rather than how you present it.

The Moon moves through a sign in about two and a quarter days, so most people can find their Rashi even without a birth time. If the Moon changed sign on your birthday, the tool will tell you both possibilities.
