The five limbs

Pancha + anga: the Hindu almanac.

A Panchang describes each day through five measures. Together they decide whether a day suits a wedding, a puja, a journey or a new venture. The values shown are those at local sunrise, as is the custom.

Tithi

The lunar day: each 12° the Moon gains on the Sun. Thirty tithis make a lunar month, fifteen waxing (Shukla) and fifteen waning (Krishna).

Vara

The weekday, ruled by the planet it is named after: Ravi (Sun), Soma (Moon), Mangal, Budh, Guru, Shukra and Shani.

Nakshatra

The lunar mansion the Moon is passing through, one of 27.

Yoga

One of 27 divisions of the combined longitude of the Sun and Moon. Some, such as Vyatipata and Vaidhriti, are avoided for new starts.

Karana

Half a tithi. Of the eleven karanas, Vishti (also called Bhadra) is avoided for auspicious work.
