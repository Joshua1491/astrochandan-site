How days are chosen

The right moment, by the old rules.

A muhurat is a time chosen so that the Moon, the lunar day and the weekday all support what you are starting. Each occasion has its own favoured Nakshatras: Rohini, Uttara Phalguni and Revati for marriage, for example, or Pushya and Hasta for a new vehicle.

This tool applies those first-pass filters at local sunrise and avoids Vishti karana. It is a reliable shortlist, but a final muhurat should also be matched to the charts of the people involved and checked for seasonal restrictions such as Chaturmas. Chandan does that in a consultation.
