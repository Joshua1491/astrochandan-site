Mangal dosha, calmly

A common placement, not a sentence.

Mangal dosha is said to form when Mars occupies the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house of the chart. Mars there is thought to bring heat and impatience into partnership, which is why it is checked before marriage.

Counted from the Lagna alone, roughly four charts in ten have it, so it is far from rare. Classical texts also list many conditions that reduce or cancel it: Mars in its own sign or exaltation, Mars aspected by Jupiter, and both partners having the dosha, among others.

That is why a dosha found by a calculator should be read in the context of the whole chart before anyone worries about it.
