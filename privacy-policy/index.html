Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Last updated 16 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It is written with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) in mind. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Chandan Shrivastava and this website.

1. Who we are

Chandan Shrivastava provides Vedic astrology consultations and arranges pujas, from Gurugram, Haryana, India. For the purposes of the DPDP Act, Chandan Shrivastava is the Data Fiduciary responsible for your personal data. You can reach us through the booking form on this website.

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to provide the service you ask for:

  • Contact details: your name and phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Birth details: date, time and place of birth, for you and, where relevant, for a partner or family member whose chart you ask us to read.
  • Your questions: what you tell us about the matters you want guidance on.
  • Puja details: names, gotra and sankalp details needed to perform a puja on your behalf.
  • Payment confirmation: the transaction reference, amount and date passed to us by the payment provider or bank. We do not receive or store your full card details, UPI PIN or banking passwords.

If you give us someone else’s birth details, please make sure they are happy for you to share them.

3. Free tools

The free tools on this website, such as the Janam Kundli, Kundli Milan, Moon sign and Sade Sati calculators, run their calculations in your own browser. The birth details you type into them are not sent to us and are not stored by us. If you want us to read a chart, you will need to share the details with us directly when you book.

4. Why we use your data

  • to arrange, prepare for and deliver your consultation;
  • to perform a puja you have booked;
  • to confirm bookings, send reminders and follow up on your consultation;
  • to confirm payments and process any refund;
  • to answer your questions and handle any complaint;
  • to meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask to receive them.

5. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you contact us or book a service, and for the specific purposes above. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us through the booking form on this website. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything done before you withdrew it, but we may then be unable to continue providing the service.

6. How long we keep it

We keep consultation records, including birth details and notes, for up to three years after your last consultation, so that follow-up sessions can build on earlier ones. You may ask us to delete them sooner. Payment records may be kept for longer where tax or accounting law requires it. When data is no longer needed, we delete it.

7. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only where needed to provide the service:

  • Pandits who perform a puja you have booked receive the names, gotra and sankalp details needed for that puja.
  • Messaging and calling providers, such as WhatsApp, carry our conversations with you, under their own privacy terms.
  • Payment providers and banks process your payments, under their own privacy terms.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires us to disclose information.

8. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage only to remember small preferences, such as the colour palette you chose and the sign you last selected for horoscopes. This information stays on your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must contact us and give consent on your behalf. Parents may of course ask us to read their child’s chart; in that case the parent provides the details and remains our point of contact.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correction and completion: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data, such as a wrong birth time.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with us and have it addressed.
  • Nomination: nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the booking form on this website. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

11. Grievances

If you have a concern about how your personal data has been handled, contact Chandan Shrivastava through the booking form on this website, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 IST. We will acknowledge your grievance within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Security

We take reasonable care to protect your data, keep access limited to those who need it, and use established messaging and payment providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we will inform you and the relevant authority if a breach affecting your data occurs, as the law requires.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. If a change materially affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you.
