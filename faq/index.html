FAQ

Questions, answered

How consultations work, how the free tools calculate, what to expect from remedies, and how your information is handled.

Consultations

How do I book a consultation?+

Use the booking form on the home page. The team gets back to you to fix a time.

Can I consult from outside India?+

Yes. Most clients abroad consult by video call. Times are arranged to suit your time zone.

How long is a session?+

Most readings run 45 to 90 minutes, depending on how many questions you bring. Short single-question sessions are also possible.

What should I have ready?+

Your date, exact time and place of birth; two or three past events with dates so the birth time can be checked; and your questions written down. For marriage matching, the same details for both people.

What are your hours?+

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 IST.

Which languages do you consult in?+

Hindi and English.

Kundli & free tools

Which method do the tools use?+

Classical Parashari Jyotish: sidereal zodiac with Lahiri ayanamsa, Vimshottari Dasha, and Ashtakoot matching for marriage.

Why is my sign different from Western astrology?+

Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac, which today sits about 24 degrees behind the tropical zodiac used in the West. Many people find their Vedic sign is the one before their Western sign.

I don't know my birth time. Can I still get a reading?+

Yes. The time can be rectified from major life events, or your question can be answered through Prashna, the chart of the moment you ask.

Remedies & pujas

Will I have to buy gemstones?+

No. A stone is suggested only if your chart clearly supports it, and Chandan does not sell gemstones.

Are pujas compulsory?+

Never. Many readings end with simple remedies you can do yourself: a mantra, a daily habit, a charity. A puja is suggested only when it fits the chart.

Can I join a puja online?+

Yes. Online pujas are performed live on video, with the sankalp taken in your name and gotra.

Privacy & payments

Is my information private?+

Yes. Your details are used only to prepare your reading and contact you about it. See the privacy policy.

How do I pay?+

The fee and payment details are shared with you when your slot is confirmed, and the session is booked once payment is received. For pujas, the dakshina is agreed in advance.

Can I cancel or reschedule?+

Yes. Rescheduling is free with 24 hours' notice. The refund and cancellation policy has the full details.
