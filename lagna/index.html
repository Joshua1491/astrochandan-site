The first house

The Lagna is the chart's point of view.

Every sign rises in turn over about 24 hours, so the Lagna changes roughly every two hours. Two people born on the same day in the same city can have very different charts because of it.

The Lagna describes the body, temperament and the general direction of life. Its ruling planet, the Lagna lord, and where that planet sits, are among the first things an astrologer looks at.

If your Lagna falls within a degree or two of a sign boundary, a few minutes' error in the birth time could change it. Chandan can rectify the time from past events in a consultation.
