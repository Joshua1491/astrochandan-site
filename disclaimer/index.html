Disclaimer

What astrology on this website can and cannot do, and how accurate the free tools are. Last updated 16 November 2026.

Please read this disclaimer before using the free tools on this website or booking a consultation or puja.

1. Astrology is guidance, not a guarantee

Vedic astrology is a traditional system of interpretation. Readings, horoscopes and articles on this website are offered as guidance for reflection, to help you think about timing, tendencies and choices. They are not statements of fact and do not guarantee any outcome. Decisions remain yours.

2. Not professional advice

Nothing on this website or in a consultation is a substitute for medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. If you have a health concern, a legal matter, a financial decision or a mental health difficulty, please consult a qualified professional. Do not stop or change any treatment because of an astrological reading. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services straight away.

3. Accuracy of the free tools

The free calculators run in your browser using standard astronomical formulas. Planetary positions are accurate to within about a degree, and the Sun and Moon to a few arc-minutes. Usually that makes no difference, but near the edge of a sign or Nakshatra it can shift the result, for example the Lagna, the Moon’s Nakshatra or the start of a Dasha. Results also depend on the birth time and place you enter. Charts prepared for consultations are checked against a full ephemeris.

4. Horoscopes

Daily, weekly and other horoscopes on this website are general, based on the Moon sign alone. They cannot account for your full birth chart or running Dasha, so they will not apply to everyone with that sign in the same way.

5. Remedies are voluntary

Any remedies suggested, such as mantras, prayers, fasting, charity or pujas, are traditional practices offered as options. They are entirely voluntary, and we do not promise any particular result from them.

6. No gemstones sold

Chandan Shrivastava does not sell gemstones, and no consultation is conditional on buying any product. If anyone claims to sell gemstones on our behalf, they are not connected with us.

7. Content and links

Articles are written for general education and reflect a classical Parashari approach. Other astrologers may interpret the same chart differently. Links to other websites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for their content.

8. Questions

If anything here is unclear, contact us through the booking form on this website.
