What it is

Seven and a half years with Saturn.

Sade Sati is the period when Saturn transits the sign before your Moon sign, your Moon sign itself, and the sign after it. Saturn spends about two and a half years in each, so the whole span is roughly seven and a half years, and it comes around about every thirty years.

Its reputation is heavier than it deserves. Saturn asks for patience, responsibility and honest effort; many people do their most solid work in Sade Sati. How it feels depends on Saturn's own strength in your chart, the strength of your Moon and the Dasha you are running.

A shorter Saturn transit, the Dhaiya, happens when Saturn is fourth from the Moon (Kantaka Shani) or eighth (Ashtama Shani). The tool flags that too. Read more about Sade Sati.
