His story

A family tradition, practised in Gurugram.

Chandan is a third-generation Jyotishi. His training comes from the Kashi (Varanasi) lineage of classical Parashari astrology, and he studied the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, the foundational text of the tradition, under his grandfather.

For more than ten years he has practised full time from Gurugram, reading charts for over 5,000 clients. He consults in Hindi and English, in person or by phone and video for families across India and abroad.

His method is classical and his manner is plain. He uses the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa and Vimshottari Dasha for timing, checks a chart against what has already happened before predicting anything, and explains his reasoning as he goes.

नमः सूर्याय सोमाय मङ्गलाय बुधाय च ।
गुरु शुक्र शनिभ्यश्च राहवे केतवे नमः ॥

Salutations to the Sun, the Moon, Mars and Mercury; to Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu.

Before your consultation

What to have ready.

  • ✓Your date of birth, and the time as exactly as you can find it: a birth certificate or hospital record is best
  • ✓Your place of birth: town or city, and district if it is small
  • ✓Two or three past events with dates (a move, a job change, a marriage) so the birth time can be verified
  • ✓Your questions written down, most important first
  • ✓For marriage matching, the same birth details for both people
  • ✓For a video session, a quiet room and a steady connection; a notebook helps
