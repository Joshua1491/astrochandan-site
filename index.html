Which system of astrology does Chandan use?+

Classical Vedic astrology in the Parashari tradition: the sidereal zodiac with Lahiri ayanamsa, Vimshottari Dasha for timing, and the eight-koota (Ashtakoot) method for marriage matching. The free tools on this site follow the same rules.

Who sees my birth details?+

Only Chandan and the small team that schedules your session. Your details are used to prepare your reading and to contact you about it, and nothing else. See the privacy policy.

What if I don't know my exact birth time?+

Tell him when you book. He can rectify the time from major events in your life, or answer your question with Prashna, the chart of the moment you ask.

How does a consultation work?+

Fill in the booking form. The team confirms a time, and you speak with Chandan by phone or video, or in person in Gurugram. Clients abroad usually join by video.

Will I be told to buy a gemstone?+

No. A stone is suggested only when your chart clearly supports it, and often the honest answer is that you don't need one. Chandan does not sell gemstones, so there is nothing to upsell.

How do I book a puja or find a muhurat?+

Pujas can be booked on the Puja Seva page, online from anywhere or at home in Gurugram. For a good date and time, try the Shubh Muhurat tool, or ask Chandan to choose one for your chart.
