Lunar mansions

Twenty-seven stars along the Moon's path.

Long before the twelve signs were used in India, the sky was divided into 27 Nakshatras, one for each day of the Moon's monthly circuit. Each has a ruling planet, a presiding deity and a symbol that colours its meaning.

Your birth Nakshatra's ruling planet is the first Mahadasha of your life. Its pada gives the syllable traditionally used to begin a name, and its Gana, Yoni and Nadi are compared when two charts are matched for marriage.

The 27 Nakshatras

Each Nakshatra spans 13°20′ of the sidereal zodiac and is divided into four padas of 3°20′. The ruling planets repeat in the Vimshottari order: Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury.
